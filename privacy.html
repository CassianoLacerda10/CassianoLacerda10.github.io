Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how Cursed Clips AutoPost ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the Cursed Clips channels to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. It is not available to the public.

Information the app accesses

  • Authorization tokens that the account owner grants through each platform's official login (Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok). They are used only to upload videos and read the status of those uploads.
  • The owner's own video files and captions, stored in the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets.
  • Basic profile information (such as account ID) returned by the platforms, used only to identify which account receives the upload.

How information is used

The information is used exclusively to publish the owner's videos and keep a record of what was published. The app does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, does not use data for advertising, and does not collect information about viewers or any other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your data at any time via the Google security settings page.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored in the owner's private configuration (a local file and encrypted repository secrets) and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's security settings, for example at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. To request deletion of any data, or if you have questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
